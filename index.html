How to Create & Install a Garmin .SRF Vehicle Icon

  1. Upload a bird's-eye car photo: A clean top-down image with a transparent or solid-color background gives the cleanest cutout.
  2. Align the vehicle direction: Use the 90° L / 90° R buttons so the front of your vehicle points straight up (North), matching the arrow overlay.
  3. Download your .SRF file: Check the 360° preview and click the red Download button to generate a ready-to-use Garmin .srf file.
  4. Install on your Garmin GPS: Connect your Garmin to your computer via USB, back up your existing \Garmin\Vehicle\ folder, and copy the new .srf file into it.
  5. Select your custom vehicle: Safely eject your GPS, then open Settings > Map & Vehicle > Vehicle on your device to choose your new icon.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Garmin models support custom .srf files? Most Garmin automotive GPS devices—including Drive, DriveSmart, DriveAssist, and older nüvi models—that contain a Vehicle folder support custom .srf vehicle icons.
Which format should I pick (v2.00 vs. v2.20)? Start with Standard 3D + 2D Overhead (v2.00) for broad compatibility. If your newer Garmin model shows a stretched or blank 2D overhead icon, switch to Newer Garmin (v2.20) and re-export.
Are my photos uploaded to a server? No. All background removal, 36-angle rotation, and binary .srf encoding happen 100% locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, tracked, or stored.
